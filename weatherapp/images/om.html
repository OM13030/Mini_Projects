The Problem

Every Indian event runs on WhatsApp groups. That's the problem.

Event managers coordinate 20–80 freelancers across decoration, catering, sound, lighting, and logistics — entirely through chat messages. When things go wrong, nobody knows until it's too late.

📱

WhatsApp chaos

200+ messages per event day. Critical updates buried. No way to know who saw what. Managers call the same person 4 times to confirm a single task.

⛓️

No dependency awareness

Catering team sets up while decoration is still running. Sound check starts before the stage is ready. Nobody sees the chain — until it collapses.

👻

Freelancer ghosting

A freelancer goes silent on event day. Manager only finds out when the task is already 45 minutes overdue and guests are waiting.

📊

Zero post-event learning

Every event ends. Nothing is documented. The same delays happen at the next event. No data, no improvement, no accountability.

💔

The real cost nobody talks about

A single botched event costs an event company ₹50,000–5,00,000 in refunds, reputation damage, and lost referrals. That's 5–10 years of EventFlow subscription wiped out in one bad day. The pain is real, recurring, and entirely preventable.

Honest Assessment

Strengths and real challenges

Built by someone from the industry — which means no sugarcoating either side.

✦ Genuine Strengths
  • Problem is real and deeply felt — every Indian event manager lives this pain daily
  • Moat is cultural knowledge, not code — extremely hard for outsiders to replicate
  • Founder has lived the problem firsthand as a freelancer and manager — unfair advantage
  • No direct vertical competitor in Gujarat — greenfield market right now
  • Pricing model charges companies not freelancers — removes biggest adoption blocker
  • PWA + WhatsApp integration removes tech barrier for older freelancers entirely
  • Seasonal demand (Nov–Feb) creates intense, concentrated word-of-mouth windows
  • Phase 3 marketplace creates network effects and switching costs simultaneously
  • Client portal is a differentiator nobody else offers — strong sales hook
  • Every event auto-generates a performance report and portfolio — compounding value
△ Real Challenges
  • Freelancer adoption is the #1 kill risk — if 60-year-old decorators won't tap, system has no data
  • Chicken-and-egg: need companies before freelancers, freelancers before marketplace
  • Gujarat SMBs may resist software subscriptions — needs careful price framing
  • Seasonal demand spikes (Nov–Feb) create infrastructure and support load challenges
  • Connectivity at venues can be poor — offline mode is essential, not optional
  • Building web dashboard + PWA + WhatsApp bot together is heavy for a small team
  • Sales is entirely relationship-driven — needs boots on ground, not digital ads
  • No brand recognition yet — first clients need founder credibility to convert

Risk Register

Known risks, honest mitigations

Freelancer adoption resistance

Your weakest link. Mitigation: WhatsApp bot as primary interface — zero new app required. PWA as upgrade path only.

Chicken-and-egg marketplace

Don't launch marketplace until 5+ companies actively use the coordination tool. Sequence is everything.

Low willingness to pay

Frame pitch as: "One saved event pays for 2 years of EventFlow." Start at ₹999/mo founding price. Raise after 10 clients.

Seasonal demand spikes

Nov–Feb is peak. Plan infrastructure for burst usage. Off-season pricing keeps clients warm through summer.

Solo build scope is heavy

Phase 1 = WhatsApp bot + web dashboard only. No PWA until Month 4. Cut scope aggressively. Ship fast, iterate faster.

Venue connectivity issues

Offline-first for freelancer PWA: status taps queue locally and sync on reconnect. Non-negotiable from Day 1.
